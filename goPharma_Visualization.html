Battery & Power Switch Wiring

  • BATT (+): Routes through the SPST Power Switch to feed both ESP32 VIN (5V) and ULN2003A 5V+.
  • BATT (-): Tied directly to ESP32 GND, ULN2003A GND, Buzzer (-), and LED Cathode (-).
  • Calibration: Technician portal (`pass: 8888`) adjusts `slotSteps` (default `134`) and `speed` (default `700` steps/s) stored in EEPROM at `0xB6`.
